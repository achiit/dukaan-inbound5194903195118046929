The House

Over 22 Years of Dressing Bhopal's Gentlemen

A family menswear house in the heart of Chowk Bazaar, Shahi Pehnawa brings together groom wear, formal tailoring and traditional ethnic wear under one roof.

From the wedding sherwani a groom remembers for a lifetime to the blazer that carries him through every formal occasion — our collection is chosen and stitched with an eye for fabric, fit and finishing.

Open every day until 10:00 PM, the store welcomes walk-ins across the week.

The Atelier

Custom Stitching, Made to Measure

Ready-to-wear is only half the story. Our in-store tailoring service shapes each garment to your measurements and posture — because a true premium silhouette is stitched, not just sold.

  • Measurement & fitting guidance at the store
  • Adjustments for sherwanis, suits and kurtas
  • Fabric selection with our team

A Note on Timelines

Every stitched order is different — fabric, season and scope all matter. Timelines and fitting schedules are discussed personally at the store when you place an order, so you always know exactly what to expect.
